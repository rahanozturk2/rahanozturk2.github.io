01 About

I forecast the weather for a living, and study the atmosphere as a doctoral researcher. Sometimes I build things for the web.

My research follows how air moves over the Black Sea, from local extremes to large-scale patterns. Alongside it I build weather and web products, drawing on an earlier life in visual design and film editing.
